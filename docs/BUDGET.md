# Bootstrap budget: launch → ~10k users

Software is built. This is **cash**, not engineering. Naira. 2026 Lagos prices. Not a fundraise deck.

**Headline**

| You mean | Lean (founders unpaid) | With 1 modest salary |
|---|---|---|
| Live (Paystack + VTpass, tokens) | **₦1.5–2.5m** | **₦3–5m** (3 months) |
| That, plus **10k registered** users | **₦12–18m** | **₦20–30m** |
| **10k people who have bought power** | **₦25–40m** | **₦35–55m** |

Working capital (VTpass wallet) sits **on top** and comes back: ~**₦2–5m** at 10k transacting-ish scale. It is not marketing spend.

Do not budget an Ikeja/MAP “API fee.” Unknown. Could be ₦0 for a pilot.

## 4. Fee leak (every sale)

On ₦5,000 of units: Paystack ~−₦175, VTpass commission ~+₦75 → **about −₦100** if you add no service fee.

At 4,000 buyers × 2 purchases/month ≈ **−₦800k/month** unless you:

- add ~₦100–150 convenience, or  
- get a better VTpass rate, or  
- accept it as CAC.

Budget that leak or price it. Do not ignore it.
